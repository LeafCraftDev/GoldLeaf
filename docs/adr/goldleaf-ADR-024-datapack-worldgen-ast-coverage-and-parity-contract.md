# goldleaf-ADR-024 — Datapack Worldgen AST Coverage, Dimension Isolation, and Parity Contract

**Status:** Accepted (Implemented)
**Date:** 2026-11-26

## Context

`goldleaf-ADR-004` and `goldleaf-ADR-017` established GoldLeaf's typed-AST-to-OpenCL worldgen pipeline (`DensityFunctionIrLowerer`, `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler`, `GpuBiomeRTree`, `GpuCarverMaskCompiler`, `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler`, `GpuFeaturePassCompiler`, `GpuOrePlacementEngine`, `OpenClKernelCache`, and `IrOpenClTerrainAccelerator`) under strict invariants: zero `FP64` in production OpenCL kernels (`INT64_Q32_FP32`, `<= 0.05%` density-boundary tolerance), zero reflection (`getDeclaredField`/`setAccessible`) in `dev.leafcraft.goldleaf.worldgen`, bounded async compilation (`S-005`), fail-closed handling on unsupported constructs (`S-004`), and zero dual execution.

A full read-only audit of MC `26.3`'s materialized worldgen AST against GoldLeaf's GPU worldgen stack (benchmarked against large real-world datapacks such as Terralith, Tectonic, and `/home/leaf26/RTP/datapacks/leaf-worldgen`, which contains `19,807` climate entries across `84` unique biomes) identified nine concrete gaps that prevented first-class GPU acceleration or risked silent semantic divergence on datapack dimensions:

1. **Three missing `DensityFunction` AST nodes (18/21 lowered):** `DensityFunctionIrLowerer` rejected `FindTopSurfaceFunction` (heavily used by Terralith/Tectonic-style datapacks), `DistanceToPointFunction`, and `EndIslandFunction` (explicitly fail-closed in `IrOpenClTerrainAcceleratorTest`), forcing custom Overworld/End datapacks onto CPU.
2. **Silent trilinear interpolation drop in `DensityFunctionIrLowerer#lowerSplitForSectionFill`:** When `CoarseChannelDiscovery` marked a tree incompatible (e.g., mixed `InterpolatedFunction` cell sizes such as `4x8` + `8x4`, non-divisor cell dimensions, or exceeding `MAX_CHANNELS`), the fallback called `lower(..., assumeAlignedCoarseGrid = true, ...)`, silently erasing trilinear interpolation instead of failing closed. `CoarseChannelDiscovery` also omitted descent into `SplineFunction`, `FindTopSurfaceFunction`, `SliceFunction`, and `RoundFunction.multiple()`.
3. **Missing `TemperatureCondition` in `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler`:** `TemperatureCondition` (`Biome#coldEnoughToSnow`) was unconditionally rejected (`"TemperatureCondition not yet supported on GPU"`), disabling GPU surface compilation for any datapack using snowline rules (`SurfaceRules.temperature()`).
4. **Header-slot collision on non-Overworld default fluids in `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler`:** `CompilationContext` registered `[AIR, defaultBlock, defaultFluid, LAVA]` via deduplicating `addBlock(...)`. In dimensions where `defaultFluid` is `AIR` (the End) or `LAVA` (the Nether), slots `2` and/or `3` were collapsed, shifting user `BlockRule` states into slot `3` — which `apply_vanilla_surface_u8`, `fill_terrain_u8`, and `pack_sections_u8` interpret as fluid/lava.
5. **Overworld-only `GpuFeaturePassCompiler` leakage in `IrOpenClTerrainAccelerator`:** When `gpuInVramFeatures` was enabled, `IrOpenClTerrainAccelerator` unconditionally passed `includeFeatureBlocks = true` to `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler.compile` (injecting 28 Overworld blocks and 21 Overworld biomes into every dimension's `u8` palette) and attempted `GpuFeaturePassCompiler.compile` (hardcoded Overworld ores/snow/grass) on non-Overworld and datapack dimensions.
6. **64-block palette cap and silent aquifer drop in `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler`:** `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler` packed block property bitmasks (`uncarvableMask`, `tickingFluidMask`, `surfOpaqueMask`, `oceanOpaqueMask`) into single 64-bit `long` words and returned `null` whenever `blockPalette.length > 64`. In `IrOpenClTerrainAccelerator`, a `null` carver/aquifer kernel silently ran `apply_vanilla_surface_u8` without aquifers on dimensions with `settings.aquifers().isPresent()`.
7. **Hardcoded vanilla Overworld `OreVeinRule` channels in `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler`:** `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler` ignored custom `OreVeinRule#density()`, `richness()`, and `fillerGap()` expressions, always binding vanilla Overworld `ORE_VEININESS/A/B/GAP` at `by = -64 + (ocy << 3)`.
8. **Silent ore drops (`S-004`) in `GpuOrePlacementEngine.CompiledOreTargets`:** `evalSlow` invoked `target.test(state, pos, null)`, swallowed `NullPointerException`, and cached only three fixed Y buckets (`<0`, `0..8`, `>8`), silently dropping ores using `RandomBlockMatchTest`, `RandomBlockStateMatchTest`, arbitrary `HeightMatchTest(min, max)`, or custom `RuleTest` subclasses.
9. **No dedicated datapack parity verification gate:** `IrOpenClTerrainAcceleratorTest` focused on vanilla Overworld terrain, leaving extended-height (`512+`), `100+`-biome, End datapack, palette-overflow, and datapack-mutation cache-invalidation contracts unverified.

## Decision

### 1. Supported MC `26.3` Datapack GPU AST Contract

GoldLeaf's typed-AST OpenCL frontend shall support the complete standard MC `26.3` datapack surface:

- **`DensityFunction` (21 / 21 vanilla nodes):** `BinaryFunction`, `UnaryFunction`, `ConstantFunction`, `YClampedGradientFunction`, `ClampFunction`, `RangeChoiceFunction`, `LerpFunction`, `IntervalSelectFunction`, `PowFunction`, `RoundFunction`, `SliceFunction`, `SplineFunction`, `NoiseFunction`, `ShiftedNoiseFunction`, `ShiftFunction`, `BlendedNoise`, `InterpolatedFunction` (uniform cell dimensions dividing chunk/section bounds), `BlendDensityFunction` / `BlendAlphaFunction` / `BlendOffsetFunction` (unblended chunk fast-path), **`DistanceToPointFunction`**, **`EndIslandFunction`**, and **`FindTopSurfaceFunction`**.
- **`MaterialCondition` (10 / 10 vanilla conditions + Paper flat bedrock):** `BiomeCondition`, `NoiseThresholdCondition`, `VerticalGradientCondition`, `YCondition`, `WaterCondition`, `StoneDepthCondition`, `SteepCondition`, `HoleCondition`, `AbovePreliminarySurfaceCondition`, `NotCondition`, and **`TemperatureCondition`**.
- **`MaterialRule` (5 / 5 vanilla rules):** `BlockRule`, `ConditionRule`, `SequenceRule`, `BandlandsRule`, and `OreVeinRule` (preserving the fast coarse-grid path for vanilla Overworld veins and lowering custom datapack vein density functions via auxiliary density evaluators).
- **Biomes:** Dynamic `MultiNoiseBiomeSource` (6-channel GPU climate + `GpuBiomeRTree` up to `256` unique biomes and `65,536` flattened R-tree nodes — covering `leaf-worldgen`'s `19,807` climate entries and Terralith-scale packs — with automatic fallback to 6-channel GPU climate + CPU R-tree above `256` biomes or `65,536` nodes).
- **Carvers & Ores:** Explicit allowlisted providers in `GpuCarverMaskCompiler` (`UniformHeight`, `ConstantInt`/`UniformInt`/`VeryBiasedToBottomInt`, `ConstantFloat`/`UniformFloat`/`TrapezoidFloat`, `genDepth <= 384`), widened `255`-block and `512`-cell / `64`-Y-band `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler` (supporting `512`-block extended-height Tectonic/Terralith dimensions up to `~640` blocks tall with lockstep `GOLDLEAF_AQ_CACHE_INTS_PER_CHUNK = 1028` in `goldleaf_opencl.c`), and strict `RuleTest` allowlisting in `GpuOrePlacementEngine`.

### 2. Exact `INT64_Q32_FP32` Lowering Specifications (Zero `FP64`, Zero Reflection)

All newly supported AST nodes lower directly into `:bytecode-api`'s existing `BytecodeConceptIr` / `OpenClBackendEmitter` primitives (`ConceptStmt.While`, `ConceptStmt.If`, `MathIntrinsicKind`, `I64`, and `INT64_Q32` helpers) with **zero `:bytecode-api` speculative extensions**, **zero `FP64`**, and **zero reflection**:

- **`DistanceToPointFunction(Vec3i point, DistanceMetric metric)`:** Emits exact `FP32` coordinate deltas `dx = (float)(point.x - bx)`, `dy = (float)(point.y - by)`, `dz = (float)(point.z - bz)` matching `DistanceToPointFunction.Sampler`, lowered per metric (`EUCLIDEAN` -> `sqrt(dx*dx + dy*dy + dz*dz)`, `EUCLIDEAN_SQUARED` -> `dx*dx + dy*dy + dz*dz`, `MANHATTAN` -> `|dx| + |dy| + |dz|`, `CHEBYSHEV` -> `max(|dx|, max(|dy|, |dz|))`).
- **`EndIslandFunction()`:** Replicates MC `26.3`'s `context.createEndIslandRandom()` + `consumeCount(17292)` + `SimplexNoise(islandRandom, true)` permutation table directly in Java (3 `nextDouble()` origin calls + 256-entry Fisher-Yates shuffle into a packed `I32` LUT, zero reflection). Lowers `getHeightValue(blockX / 8, blockZ / 8)` with truncating integer division (`sectionX = bx / 8`, `chunkX = sectionX / 2`, `subSectionX = sectionX % 2`, `xo, zo in [-12, 12]`), `I64` radius gate (`tx*tx + tz*tz > 4096L`), and **`INT64_Q32` 2D Simplex skew/unskew** (`F2_Q32`, `G2_Q32`) so large world coordinates (`+-100k..30M`) remain drift-free without `FP64`. Computes `islandSize = (|(float)tx|*3439.0f + |(float)tz|*147.0f) % 13.0f + 9.0f`, `clamp(100 - sqrt(xd*xd + zd*zd)*islandSize, -100, 80)`, and `(doffs - 8.0f) / 128.0f`.
- **`FindTopSurfaceFunction(density, upperBound, lowerBound, cellHeight)`:** Rejects `cellHeight <= 0` (fail-closed). Lowers `upperBound` at `y = 0` (matching MC `26.3`'s `SliceFunction.YSampler(sampler, 0)` wrapping) and lowers `density` as an isolated helper `ConceptFunction(bx, probeY, bz)` (preserving DAG CSE while cleanly varying `probeY`). Emits a `ConceptStmt.While` loop stepping `y = floor(upperBound / cellHeight) * cellHeight` downward by `cellHeight` to `lowerBound`, returning the first `y` with `density(bx, y, bz) > 0.0f` or `(float)lowerBound`.
- **`TemperatureCondition` (`Biome#coldEnoughToSnow`):** Reads `Biome.climateSettings` directly via public MC `26.3` accessors (`temperature()`, `temperatureModifier()` in `{NONE, FROZEN}`) with zero reflection. Emits `gl_simplex_temp_perm` (seeded from `LegacyRandomSource(1234L)`, matching `Biome.TEMPERATURE_NOISE`), `__constant float gl_biome_base_temp[256]`, and a 256-bit `ulong[4]` frozen-modifier bitmask (reusing `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler`'s existing `gl_frozen_ocean_should_melt` helper for `TemperatureModifier.FROZEN`). Evaluates `snowLevel = seaLevel + 17`, applies `(v + by - snowLevel) * 0.05f / 40.0f` when `by > snowLevel`, and tests `< 0.15f`. Unreferenced biomes from `BiomeSource#possibleBiomes()` are deduplicated by `(Float.floatToIntBits(baseTemp), isFrozen)` into shared temperature-class IDs so large datapacks with 100+ biomes do not exhaust `MAX_BIOME_PALETTE = 254` unless >254 distinct biome keys or temperature classes are actually needed.
- **Custom `OreVeinRule` & Legacy RNG (`LegacyRandomSource`) lowering:** Retains the fast coarse-grid `ore_vein_a`/`ore_vein_b` path exclusively when the rule matches vanilla Overworld `ORE_VEININESS`/`A`/`B`/`GAP` with `minY == -64`; lowers arbitrary custom datapack `OreVeinRule` density functions via `addAuxDensity` (failing closed if an unlifted `InterpolatedFunction` appears). Supports both `XoroshiroRandomSource` and `LegacyRandomSource` (`LegacyPositionalRandomFactory`) in `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler` (`VerticalGradientCondition`, `OptionallyFlatBedrockCondition`, `OreVeinRule`, and surface depth noise), plus non-aquifer `NETHER_CAVE` carving in `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler` / `GpuCarverMaskCompiler`, and precomputed `invFudgeYScale = 1.0 / fudgeYScale` reciprocal multiplication in `DensityFunctionIrLowerer`'s `gl_perlin_3d_smeared` helper so `INT64_Q32_FP32` preserves exact integer quotients at coarse Nether/End Y slices without FP32 division round-down.

### 3. Palette Scaling, Header-Slot Isolation, and Overworld Gating

- **Fixed Header Slots (`0..3`) in `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler`:** Unconditionally reserve `blockPalette[0..3] = [AIR, defaultBlock, defaultFluid, LAVA]` without collapsing duplicate states (`blockToId.putIfAbsent`), and compile-time guard fluid classification (`defaultFluidIsFluid = !defaultFluid.getFluidState().isEmpty()`) so user `BlockRule` states start at index `>= 4` and never collide with slot `2`/`3` in the End or Nether.
- **`u8` Palette Retention & `ulong[4]` Carver/Aquifer Bitmasks:** Keep `u8` voxel/biome storage (`MAX_BLOCK_PALETTE = 255`, `MAX_BIOME_PALETTE = 254`, `GpuBiomeRTree.MAX_PALETTE_SIZE = 256`) to preserve `goldleaf-ADR-017`'s `<= 4 KiB/section` PCIe DMA and `.lca` memory envelope. Widen `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler`'s `uncarvableMask`, `tickingFluidMask`, `surfOpaqueMask`, and `oceanOpaqueMask` from single `ulong` (64 blocks) to `ulong[4]` (all `255` `u8` surface blocks). If `settings.aquifers().isPresent()` and `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler.compile` fails or returns `null`, `IrOpenClTerrainAccelerator` fails closed on the vanilla-surface path (falling back cleanly to 2-bit terrain + CPU `buildSurface`/aquifers) so aquifers are never silently dropped.
- **Strict Overworld Gating of `GpuFeaturePassCompiler`:** Gate both `includeFeatureBlocks` in `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler.compile` and `GpuFeaturePassCompiler.compile` strictly to vanilla Overworld (`Level.OVERWORLD` with vanilla Overworld height/settings), preventing 28 Overworld blocks and 21 Overworld biomes from bloating or corrupting datapack and non-Overworld dimensions.
- **Strict `RuleTest` Allowlist in `GpuOrePlacementEngine`:** Add `isStaticTargetCacheSafe(OreFeature)` allowing only deterministic, RNG-free, 3-bucket-compatible tests (`AlwaysTrueTest`, `BlockMatchTest`, `BlockStateMatchTest`, `TagMatchTest`, vanilla `0..8` `HeightMatchTest`, and `AllOfRuleTest`/`AnyOfRuleTest`/`NotRuleTest` composed of them). Any `OreFeature` with `RandomBlockMatchTest`, `RandomBlockStateMatchTest`, arbitrary `HeightMatchTest`, or custom `RuleTest` bypasses the static target cache and executes via CPU `placer.placeWithBiomeCheck` in strict feature-over-feature order.

### 4. Cache-Key Provenance Contract

- **Disk `.clbin` Cache (`OpenClKernelCache`):** Cache keys are content-addressed via `SHA-256(deviceSignature, buildOpts, mode + emittedOpenClSource + spilledLutWords)`. Because every datapack-defining input (AST structure, noise parameters and seeds, `NoiseGeneratorSettings` bounds/seaLevel/defaultBlock/defaultFluid, surface rule tree, block/biome palettes, biome temperature LUTs, flat-bedrock flag, and macro-noise flag) is baked directly into the emitted OpenCL source and spilled LUT words, any datapack edit or reload automatically produces a distinct digest and never serves a stale `.clbin`.
- **In-Memory Compilation Maps (`IrOpenClTerrainAccelerator`):** Keyed by dimension identifier, world seed, `NoiseGeneratorSettings` identity and structural hash (`settings.hashCode()`), and `ParityMode`, preventing accidental hash collisions across in-process datapack reloads.

### 5. Intentional Fail-Closed Boundary (`S-004`)

The following constructs remain intentionally CPU-evaluated with single-shot structured diagnostic logging, zero dual execution, and zero silent half-GPU corruption:

- Third-party Java `ChunkGenerator` subclasses (not `NoiseBasedChunkGenerator`).
- Non-`MultiNoiseBiomeSource` providers (`TheEndBiomeSource`, `FixedBiomeSource`, `CheckerboardColumnBiomeSource`) — already microseconds on CPU and bypassed in `isEligibleForGpuBiomes`.
- Unrecognized `PositionalRandomFactory` implementations (outside `XoroshiroRandomSource` and `LegacyRandomSource`) in `MaterialRule`/`OreVeinRule`, and unkeyed `Holder.direct(Biome)` in `BiomeCondition`.
- Dimensions exceeding `255` distinct surface `BlockState`s or `254` distinct referenced biome/temperature IDs (graceful dimension-level fallback from `u8` GPU surface to 2-bit GPU terrain + CPU `buildSurface`), or `>256` unique biomes in `MultiNoiseBiomeSource` (graceful fallback from `GpuBiomeRTree` to 6-channel GPU climate + CPU R-tree).
- Mixed or non-divisor `InterpolatedFunction` cell sizes (`lowerSplitForSectionFill` now passes `assumeAlignedCoarseGrid = false` on fallback so unlifted `InterpolatedFunction` fails closed cleanly instead of silently dropping trilinear interpolation).
- Unrecognized custom `DensityFunction`, `MaterialRule`, `MaterialCondition`, `Noise`, `WorldCarver`, `HeightProvider`/`IntProvider`/`FloatProvider` (e.g., `BiasedToBottomHeight`, `TrapezoidHeight`, `WeightedListInt`, `ClampedNormalFloat`), or `genDepth > 384` in `GpuCarverMaskCompiler` (falls back cleanly to CPU carver mask).
- Non-empty `Blender` chunks (legacy chunk boundary blending).

## Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Why Rejected |
|-------------|--------------|
| Lift arbitrary datapack `DensityFunction`s via JVM bytecode scanning in `:bytecode-api` | Fragile across MC refactors and obfuscation, complicates security/fail-closed auditing, and unnecessary because MC `26.3` exposes all 21 standard `DensityFunction` records cleanly as typed AST nodes (`goldleaf-ADR-017`). |
| Widen surface voxel & biome buffers from `u8` to `u16` (`65,535` blocks/biomes) | Doubles VRAM footprint, PCIe DMA readback bandwidth (`4 KiB -> 8 KiB/section`), and `.lca` direct-serialization buffer pressure for every vanilla and normal datapack chunk, while requiring invasive rewrites across `goldleaf_opencl.c` and five OpenCL kernels. Deduplicating unreferenced biome temperatures plus `ulong[4]` aquifer masks handles Terralith/Tectonic-scale datapacks within `u8`, while truly massive `>255`-block packs fail closed cleanly to 2-bit GPU terrain + CPU `buildSurface`. |
| Use `FP64` (`double`) in `EndIslandFunction` 2D Simplex noise | Violates `goldleaf-ADR-017`'s zero-`FP64` invariant (`1/64` throughput cliff on consumer GeForce GPUs such as RTX 3090). `INT64_Q32` skew/unskew achieves bit-stable large-coordinate accuracy at full integer/FP32 throughput. |
| Silently run `apply_vanilla_surface_u8` without aquifers when `GpuCarverAquiferCompiler` returns `null` | Violates `S-004` by silently flooding underground caves below sea level with water instead of vanilla aquifers. Widening masks to `ulong[4]` plus failing closed on `compiledCarver == null` eliminates the silent corruption path. |

## Forward-Porting Notes (MC `26.4+`)

MC `26.4` is expected to evolve worldgen composition (e.g., `UpscaleFunction` replacing or generalizing `InterpolatedFunction`, `ChunkTerrainBuilder`, and `SurfaceExtensions`). Keeping all lowering confined to GoldLeaf's typed AST frontends (`DensityFunctionIrLowerer`, `GpuVanillaSurfaceCompiler`) with zero reflection and zero `:bytecode-api` MC-specific coupling ensures MC `26.4` adaptation is localized to record pattern matches in the frontend classes rather than kernel emitter rewrites.

## Consequences

- **Positive:**
  - 21/21 MC `26.3` `DensityFunction` nodes, 10/10 `MaterialCondition`s, and 5/5 `MaterialRule`s execute natively on OpenCL GPUs under `INT64_Q32_FP32` with zero `FP64` and zero reflection.
  - Terralith/Tectonic-style extended-height (`512+`) Overworld datapacks, `100+`-biome climate lists, and custom End datapacks achieve first-class GPU acceleration with verified 1:1 CPU parity (`<= 0.05%` density-boundary tolerance).
  - Eliminates silent corruption bugs across End/Nether header slots, Overworld feature leakage, mixed `InterpolatedFunction` fallback, 65..255-block aquifer drops, custom `OreVeinRule`s, and `RandomBlock*MatchTest` ores.
  - `DatapackWorldgenParityTest` enforces an automated regression and live-GPU parity gate for datapack worldgen.
- **Negative / Trade-offs:**
  - Dimensions exceeding `255` distinct surface `BlockState`s or `254` distinct referenced biome/temperature classes continue to use 2-bit GPU terrain + CPU `buildSurface` rather than full `u8` GPU surface.
  - `GpuCarverMaskCompiler` retains its `MAX_GEN_DEPTH = 384` bound; taller `512`-block dimensions fall back cleanly to CPU carver mask generation while keeping GPU terrain, biomes, and surface.
  - `FindTopSurfaceFunction` executes a bounded column loop per work-item; datapacks with very small `cellHeight` over tall vertical spans incur proportionally higher per-voxel ALU cost on GPU, though still substantially faster than CPU evaluation.
